**Critical PHP “php2shell” RCE Vulnerabilities Prompt Immediate Patch**  
What happened: Two unauthenticated remote‑code‑execution flaws in PHP (CVE‑2026‑109618 and CVE‑2026‑109619), dubbed “php2shell,” were disclosed on 21 Oct 2026, affecting all PHP 5+ versions; patches were released in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, and 8.2.35.  
Why you should care: Most web‑development projects in Indian diploma IT curricula use PHP; unpatched installations can be compromised, leading to loss of data, reputation, and potential legal issues for student projects or internships.  
Impact: Medium  
What to do: Verify your development environment’s PHP version and upgrade immediately to one of the patched releases; review any live‑site deployments and apply the update before the next build.
