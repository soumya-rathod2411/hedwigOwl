## Cybersecurity
**Critical PHP Vulnerabilities (CVE‑2026‑109618 & CVE‑2026‑109619) – Immediate Patch Required**  
What happened: Two high‑severity remote‑code‑execution flaws were disclosed in core PHP, rapidly added to the U.S. CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog and confirmed exploited in the wild.  
Why you should care: PHP powers many Indian web‑development projects and college labs; unpatched servers can be hijacked, leading to data loss or service disruption for coursework and internships.

**Critical Node.js Brotli Decoder Vulnerability (CVE‑2026‑111497) – Patch Now**  
What happened: A critical flaw (CVSS 9.8) was found in Node.js’s Brotli decoder component, allowing remote code execution. The Cyber Security Agency of Singapore released an emergency update.  
Why you should care: Node.js is widely used in modern JavaScript back‑ends and many student projects; exploitation can compromise APIs and services you develop for assignments or campus hackathons.  
Impact: Medium  
What to do: Update Node.js to the latest stable release (v20.x or later) that includes the fix; test your applications after upgrade and review dependency lock‑files for vulnerable packages.  
Source: Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-154)
